## Why this site exists

Social platforms are good for fragments, but not for durable thinking. I wanted a space I truly own—a place for projects, technical decisions, and ideas worth revisiting.

## The first-version rule

The first version follows three principles: fast, simple, and maintainable. It stays static, avoids accounts and tracking, and keeps every article in Markdown.

## What comes next

I will write about web development, product design, and calmer ways of working. First, ship it. Then let it grow into the right shape.
